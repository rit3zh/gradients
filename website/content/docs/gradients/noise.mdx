Noise samples layered simplex noise across the view and maps the result onto your color ramp. With two or three dark colors it becomes a cloudy, moody background; with black and white at low opacity it's a texture for [stacks](/docs/components/gradient-stack).

`scale` zooms the field, `octaves` adds finer detail, and `warp` feeds the noise back into itself for swirled, marbled shapes. `contrast` stretches the field across more of the ramp.

## Motion

Noise is still by default (`speed` is 0). Any `speed` above zero makes the field evolve slowly through time; values around `0.2` to `0.4` look like drifting cloud.

`seed` picks a different region of the noise field, so several noise layers don't look identical.
