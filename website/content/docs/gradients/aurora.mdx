Aurora draws up to six bands of light, each a curtain with a bright lower edge, soft rays rising above it and a slow ripple along its length. Each band takes the next part of your palette.

The layer is transparent where there's no light, so it composes naturally over a dark backdrop, a [sky](/docs/gradients/sky), or a photo with `blendMode="screen"`.

/>

`softness` controls how gently each curtain fades at its lower edge, and `intensity` brightens the whole display.
