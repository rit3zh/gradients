<GradientExample type="reflected" />

The gradient runs from the mirror line outward in both directions, so the first color sits in the middle and the last at both edges. Unlike repeating a linear gradient, the fold is rounded: `softness` keeps the mirror line from showing a crease.

`offset` slides the mirror line along the axis, from `-1` to `1`, and `angle` or `start`/`end` orient it like a [linear gradient](/docs/gradients/linear).

  ]}
/>

It's a natural fit for horizon lines, light bars and the sheen across a button.
