<GradientExample type="sweep" />

Where a conic always covers a full turn, a sweep spreads its colors between `startAngle` and `endAngle`. The gap between the ends is split: the half nearer the end keeps the last color, the half nearer the start keeps the first. That gives a clean arc with a hard edge where the gap's halves meet.

  ]}
/>

Swapping the angles reverses the direction. Use it for gauges, progress rings and radar-style scanners with `spin`.
