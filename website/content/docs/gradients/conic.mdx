Colors sweep clockwise around `center`, starting at `angle` (0 is straight up). The default palette repeats its first color at the end so the turn closes without a seam; do the same with your own colors unless you want a hard edge.

```tsx
<ConicGradient colors={['#FC00FF', '#7303C0', '#FF0080', '#FC00FF']} />
```

`AngularGradient` is the same component under another name, for anyone coming from design tools that call it that.

## Motion

`spin` turns the whole wheel. It's the classic animated border when used inside a [`GradientBorder`](/docs/components/gradient-border). `flow` cycles the colors around a still wheel instead.
