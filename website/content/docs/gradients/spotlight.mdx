Spotlight casts a cone from `origin`, pointing along `angle` (180 is straight down). The light is brightest at the source, falls off along the beam and fades at the cone's edges by `softness`. Outside the beam it's transparent.

`spread` is the cone's width in degrees and `length` its reach, as a fraction of the view's diagonal.

## Motion

With `speed` above zero the beam sways gently from side to side, like a stage light. `spin` turns it steadily instead.
