<GradientExample type="flux" />

Flux swirls the view around its center and adds a layer of fine ripples, then blends your palette across two soft diagonals. It works best with two to four colors and gives a warm, organic movement that never repeats exactly.

`turbulence` controls the swirl, `ripple` the fine waves on top, `scale` the size of both.
