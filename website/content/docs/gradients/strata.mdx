Strata draws up to twelve undulating ridges, one behind the other, and colors each layer from the next part of the palette. Light falls from the top (`shade`), so the lower, nearer layers read darker, like a landscape seen at dusk.

`count` sets the number of ridges, `frequency` how often they undulate and `angle` their tilt.
