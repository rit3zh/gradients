Vignette darkens the edges and leaves the center transparent. It's rarely used alone: put it last in a [stack](/docs/components/gradient-stack), or over an image, to draw the eye inward.

`radius` is where darkening begins and `softness` how wide the falloff is. `roundness` shapes it: `1` is round, `0` follows the rectangle of the view. `color` sets the edge color; any color works, so a warm vignette is one prop away. For more control, `colors` replaces the transparent-to-`color` ramp entirely.

Vignettes are static, and cheap to add to any stack.
