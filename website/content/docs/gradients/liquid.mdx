Liquid warps noise through noise twice over, which folds the color ramp into flowing ribbons and eddies. A glossy `highlight` rides the ridges, so it reads as a wet, reflective surface.

`warp` sets how tightly the flow folds, `scale` how large the eddies are.

Liquid moves on its own. `speed` around `0.4` to `0.6` keeps it hypnotic rather than busy.
