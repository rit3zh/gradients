Interlace maps your palette over a folded sheet and weaves it with hundreds of fine lines. Alternate lines are slightly darker, which gives a printed, almost textile look.

The palette repeats across the sheet (`tileMode="repeat"`), so close it by repeating the first color at the end. Otherwise you'll see a hard seam where the last color meets the first.

```tsx
<InterlaceGradient colors={['#4568DC', '#B06AB3', '#FF5F6D', '#4568DC']} />
```

`density` sets the number of lines, `depth` their contrast and `frequency` how tightly the sheet folds.

<Swatches

  ]}
/>

It renders at native resolution so the lines stay crisp.
