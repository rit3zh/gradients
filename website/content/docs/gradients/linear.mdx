title: "Linear"

<GradientExample type="linear" />

The first color sits at the start of the line and the last at the end. With `angle`, the line runs through the center and is just long enough to reach both corners, the same geometry as CSS: `180` runs top to bottom, `90` left to right.

For exact placement, pass `start` and `end` as unit points instead. They take precedence over `angle`.

```tsx
<LinearGradient colors={['#12C2E9', '#F64F59']} start={[0, 0]} end={[1, 1]} />
```

## Motion

`spin` rotates the line in degrees per second; with points, they rotate around the center. `flow` scrolls the colors along the line.
