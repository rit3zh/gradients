The simplest surface: four colors, one per corner, in the order top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. Fewer colors repeat to fill the corners.

`smoothness` eases the blend. At `0` it's a straight bilinear interpolation; at `1` each corner holds its color a little longer before blending, which avoids the faint cross that linear blending can show.

/>

Bilinear gradients never animate on their own, so they're ideal for large static surfaces. Pair them with a [transition](/docs/guides/transitions) to shift the corners on state changes.
