Iridescent simulates light interfering in a thin film: color depends on the film's thickness and the angle you look from, which is why it shifts across the surface and with tilt. The default spectrum is pearly and pastel.

With `colors`, the film blends toward your palette. `paletteMix` controls how much, from pure physics (`0`) to fully your colors (`1`); it defaults to `0.7` when colors are given.

Turn on `deviceMotion` and the film responds to tilt, which is striking on cards and hero images.
