Each color owns a cell: the region of the view closest to its point. `smoothness` decides how the cells meet, from crisp edges to a gentle melt.

Without `points`, `cells` points are scattered from `seed`. Colors cycle over the cells, so five colors across nine cells repeat nicely.

  ]}
/>

With `drift`, the cell centers wander and the borders shift with them, like a slow lava lamp.
